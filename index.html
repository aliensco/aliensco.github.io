About

My research is driven by a simple question: can intelligent systems move beyond producing plausible outputs to form grounded, causal, and verifiable models of the world?

I am especially excited by multimodal foundation models, embodied intelligence, causal video world models, and trustworthy knowledge reasoning. I want to connect rigorous research with real systems—using physical dynamics, evidence, and human feedback to make AI more capable, reliable, and useful.

Mathematics is central to how I think about intelligence. My minor in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics gives me foundational tools for studying learning, optimization, uncertainty, and causality. I enjoy moving from precise mathematical abstractions to testable AI models, while my training in business administration helps me connect technical ideas with real needs, products, and long-term impact.

01

T2K

Thread-to-Knowledge

Designing a knowledge production engine that transforms community threads, reviews, products, services, and web content into structured, verifiable knowledge for the Yeegoods campus platform.

T2K extracts atomic claims and their evidence spans, normalizes entities, aspects, time, and conditions, and identifies support, conflict, update, and similarity relations. Human review, provenance tracking, and graph verification keep downstream retrieval and question answering grounded in traceable evidence.

  • Knowledge Graphs
  • Trustworthy RAG
  • Graph Reasoning
  • Natural Language Processing

02

EverydayBifurcationBench

Physical World Modeling Benchmark

Leading the development of a benchmark for evaluating whether video generation models can understand and reproduce physical causality in realistic everyday scenes.

The project builds controllable, paired counterfactual scenarios around six physical mechanisms—including sliding, toppling, support failure, passage constraints, edge falling, and collision propagation—and diagnoses where a model first departs from a valid causal event chain.

  • World Models
  • Physical Reasoning
  • Video Generation
  • Simulation-to-Real
